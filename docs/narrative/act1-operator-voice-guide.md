# Act 1 Operator Voice Guide Draft

## Purpose

This document defines first-pass Act 1 launch voice guidance for 15 operators: 5 initial operators and 10 gacha elite operators.

The text here is narrative copy guidance only. It does not define operator stats, skill behavior, gacha probability, reward eligibility, server truth, Unity implementation contracts, or ownership guarantees.

## Launch Guardrails

- Keep battle callouts short enough for portrait mobile combat UI.
- Use these cues as string-id candidates later; do not treat this draft as finalized localization data.
- Skill and ultimate lines are short cues only. Long ultimate movies are outside Act 1 launch scope.
- Recruitment language should imply assignment, rescue, defection, response to signal, or voluntary boarding. Do not describe operators as purchased, owned, guaranteed, or rewarded people.
- Do not reference Rail Choice Events in launch copy.
- Do not reference World Raid, ranking, settlement, score brackets, seasons, or leaderboards in launch copy.
- Do not include gacha rates, pity promises, ticket quantities, reward amounts, ledger behavior, or server calculation details in profile/codex text.
