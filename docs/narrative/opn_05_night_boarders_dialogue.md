# opn_05_night_boarders Dialogue Draft

## Purpose

`opn_05_night_boarders` brings the Raider threat inside the train. The mission turns passengers into a community that can defend itself.

The mission text should make the player feel three things:

1. The train is no longer only a battlefield around the tracks. It is home, and home is being invaded.
2. Fear can make people suspect each other, but trust can be organized.
3. Last Rail becomes stronger when passengers stop being cargo and start becoming crew.

## Tone Rules

- Keep callouts urgent and spatial: carriage, engine, infirmary, doors, lights.
- Use claustrophobic language. This mission should feel tighter than prior route defense.
- Avoid making passengers sound instantly brave. Courage should feel improvised.
- Raiders should sound less theatrical than `opn_04`; inside the train they are practical, cruel, and close.
- The Conductor should feel personally responsible for every carriage.
