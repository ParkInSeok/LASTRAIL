# opn_04_scrapstar_show Dialogue Draft

## Purpose

`opn_04_scrapstar_show` is where the Raiders become more than hostile scavengers. They reveal their philosophy in public: fear is order, mercy is waste, and survival should be entertainment for those with fuel.

The mission text should make the player feel three things:

1. The Raiders are selling permission to stop caring.
2. Saving people during combat is harder than simply winning.
3. Last Rail's answer to spectacle is interruption.

## Tone Rules

- Raider lines can be theatrical, cruel, and performative.
- Last Rail lines should stay controlled; do not let the heroes sound like the same kind of show.
- Keep combat callouts short enough for mobile UI.
- Use broadcast language: audience, program, featured prize, signal, interruption.
- Avoid making rescued people feel like rewards or inventory.
