# raid_raider_king_engine Dialogue Draft

## Purpose

`raid_raider_king_engine` is the Act 1 raid and the answer to the first signal. The player has inherited the warning not to save the engine and abandon the people. The Raider King's Engine is the opposite philosophy made physical: a train that keeps moving by making everything around it disposable.

The raid text should make the player feel three things:

1. The boss is not only a machine. It is a system of fear, trophies, fuel, and obedience.
2. Winning means stopping the engine without becoming like it.
3. Act 1 ends with Last Rail heavier, slower, and more worth defending.

## Tone Rules

- Keep raid callouts short enough for combat UI.
- Let the Raider King speak in ruthless efficiency language.
- Let the Conductor answer with choices that sound impractical, human, and now inevitable.
- Let the Signal Officer carry the transition from receiving the first signal to becoming a signal for others.
- Let the Chief Mechanic focus on heat, couplers, fuel core pressure, and the danger of destroying too much.
- Avoid trophy/loot language for rescued people. People are freed, boarded, or protected, not acquired.

## Post-Mission Result Text

### Clear Title

왕관 기관차

### Clear Summary

Last Rail은 왕관 기관차를 정지시키고, 포로칸과 중계차를 분리했다. 약탈왕의 방송은 끊겼지만, 그 자리에 Last Rail의 신호가 남았다. 첫 신호는 끝났고, 이제 열차가 다음 신호를 보낸다.

### First Clear Reward Framing

왕관 기관차의 지휘 기록과 보존된 경로 자료를 회수했습니다. 해방된 객차의 승선 지원 체계가 Last Rail에 합류했습니다.

### Repeat Clear Reward Framing

정지된 왕관 기관차 주변 노선에서 유지 자재와 경로 데이터를 회수했습니다.

## Implementation Notes

- These are narrative text candidates, not finalized localized string IDs.
- Use stable mission id `raid_raider_king_engine` for data references.
- This raid should close Act 1 and seed Act 2 through outgoing signals, not through reward quantities.
- Do not encode reward quantities or economy values in narrative text.
- First clear reward language should align with recovered route records, freed support systems, and one-time preserved command data.
- Repeat reward language should align with patrol, stabilization, and route data recovery, not pillage.
- Captives and rescued specialists should never be described as purchased goods or owned rewards.
- Optional objective language can support future prisoner car, broadcast relay, supply car, fuel core, or engine integrity scoring without requiring it in launch scope.
