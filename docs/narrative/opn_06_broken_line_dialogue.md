# opn_06_broken_line Dialogue Draft

## Purpose

`opn_06_broken_line` is the final standard operation of Act 1. It reveals that the first signal was not a living rescue request, but a message left by people who held the line before Last Rail arrived.

The mission text should make the player feel three things:

1. The broken line is a wound in the world, not just a blocked route.
2. Repairing the line is an act of inheritance.
3. The Raider King's Engine is no longer a rumor. It is close.

## Tone Rules

- Keep combat callouts short and high-pressure.
- Use repair, bridge, rail, and signal language.
- Let the Chief Mechanic carry the physical impossibility of the crossing.
- Let the Signal Officer carry the emotional weight of the recovered message.
- Let the Conductor recognize that stopping for others has become Last Rail's identity.
