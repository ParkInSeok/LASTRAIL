Source files remain unchanged under sources/. source-inventory.json records raw SHA256; audio-manifest.json records every selected excerpt, source hash, start, duration, gain, EQ, placement and output hash. build_audio.py is the exact local edit recipe. No pitch shifting or the old combat_skill/heal/repair waveforms are used.

No speech, music, newly invented weapon type or gameplay damage is introduced by these files. A chosen rifle recording is a timbre source, not a canonical real-world weapon assignment to a character.

Quality boundary: original firearm source author explicitly noted recording-volume limitations. Transients were isolated from ambient lead-in; this is not proof that recording distortion has been removed. Mono 48kHz 16-bit PCM exports are decoded and inspected for finite values, duration, unique hashes, digital clipping and clean endpoints. None of those tests are a hearing test. Subjective listening and actual Unity device mix remain NOT PERFORMED. The current interface provides no tool returning audible observations; the candidate reel is delivered for human audition, and quality remains open. Never mark sound quality approved from this document.
